Privacy Policy — V.R World Media

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Contact for privacy requests: visionaryrealworld@gmail.com

1. Who we are

V.R World Media ("we", "us") is a media company publishing short-form video brands and email newsletters. This policy covers information collected through our email lists, lead magnets, and websites, and — in section 12 — data handled by our internal posting dashboard through YouTube API Services.

2. What we collect

  • Email address — when you subscribe to a newsletter, request a lead magnet (checklist, template, PDF), or enter via a comment-to-DM flow.
  • Consent record — when and how you opted in (timestamp, source such as "Instagram DM keyword HEIST", signup form, or referral).
  • Name — only if you provide it (optional on our forms).
  • Engagement data — which emails you click. (We do not rely on open tracking; Apple's Mail Privacy Protection makes open rates unreliable, so we measure clicks.)
  • Technical data — if you visit our websites: pages viewed, device type. See section 8 on analytics.

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn we have, we delete it.

3. Why we collect it (purpose)

  • To send the newsletters and content you signed up for.
  • To deliver lead magnets and transactional messages (welcome sequences, delivery confirmations).
  • To measure which content is useful (click data) and keep our lists healthy.
  • To comply with email law (consent and unsubscribe records).

We use your data only for these purposes. We do not use it for anything else without asking first.

4. Consent and opt-in

  • You join our lists by an affirmative act: submitting a signup form, replying with your email in a DM flow, or clicking a confirmed opt-in.
  • We keep a timestamped record of every opt-in (who, when, how).
  • We never buy email lists, scrape addresses, or add you because you follow us on social media. Following ≠ subscribing.
  • Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it — see section 5.

5. Unsubscribe — your opt-out rights

Every marketing email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also reply "unsubscribe" to any email.

  • We honor unsubscribe requests within 10 business days as required by U.S. CAN-SPAM law; in practice we aim for 48 hours.
  • Unsubscribing is free and requires no extra information from you.
  • After you unsubscribe, we keep only the minimal record needed to prove we honored your request (your address on a suppression list) — you will not be mailed again.

6. Data retention

  • Active subscribers: we keep your email address while you are subscribed.
  • Unengaged subscribers: addresses with no clicks for 120+ days are suppressed (removed from mailing) as part of list hygiene.
  • Consent and suppression records: kept up to 3 years as legal proof of compliance, then deleted.
  • Deletion requests: honored within 30 days; we confirm by email when done.

7. No sale of your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your email address with advertisers or data brokers. The only parties that touch your data are:

  • Our email service provider, which stores and sends mail on our behalf under its own privacy terms and authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
  • Authorities, only if legally compelled.

8. Cookies and analytics

Our websites do not run Google Analytics — or any third-party analytics — without your prior, explicit consent. If we ever add analytics, it will sit behind a consent banner, load only after you opt in, and this section will be updated to name the tool, what it collects, and how to withdraw consent.

9. Security

  • Email sending is handled by our provider with domain authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
  • Access to subscriber data is limited to the founder and the tools that need it to send mail.
  • API keys and credentials are never exposed in website code.
  • No system is perfectly secure; if we ever suffer a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including EU/UK under GDPR and California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to: see what data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, take it with you (portability), and object to how we use it. Email the contact above and we will respond within 30 days. We will never discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will note the change here with a new effective date and — for significant changes — tell you by email before it takes effect.

12. Addendum: YouTube API Services and our posting dashboard

Our internal posting dashboard uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to V.R World Media's own YouTube channels. By using YouTube, channel owners and viewers are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy

What API data we access. When the dashboard's operator (the founder of V.R World Media) authorizes the dashboard with his Google account, the dashboard accesses, via the YouTube Data API v3: channel identifiers and names for the four channels he owns, and the upload functionality needed to publish videos (video file, title, description, tags, and privacy status) that he creates and approves in the dashboard.

How we use it. Solely to upload the operator's own original videos to his own channels and to show him his channels' basic information inside his local dashboard. We do not use API data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating our own channels.

How it is stored. The dashboard runs locally on the operator's own computer. OAuth tokens and any API data are stored only on that machine. API data is not stored on external servers, not synced to cloud services by the dashboard, and not accessed by anyone other than the operator.

How it is shared. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share YouTube API data with any third party. API data leaves the operator's machine only in direct calls to Google's YouTube API endpoints for the upload operations described above.

Retention and deletion. Locally stored API data (channel metadata and upload records) is kept only while the dashboard is in use and is deleted when the dashboard's local data store is deleted. The operator can delete it at any time by removing the dashboard's local data files.

Revoking access. The operator can revoke the dashboard's access to his Google account at any time via Google's security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions — in addition to the controls described elsewhere in this policy. Revoking access stops all further API use; it does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube, which the operator manages in YouTube Studio.
